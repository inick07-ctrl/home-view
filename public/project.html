Where to get parcel data

Most US counties have a free GIS parcel viewer. Search for:

search "your county state GIS parcel viewer"

You can also use a plat from the county assessor or recorder, a survey of the lot, and the local zoning setbacks from the county or city zoning office. Export the parcel as GeoJSON or KML if the viewer offers it, or type the corner coordinates or survey calls below. Some counties list plats and parcel documents in a land-records portal (sometimes called Datafi) — search for your county’s Datafi or document search and use the recorded survey or plat there.
