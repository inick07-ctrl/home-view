Room blocks

Pick a size, then drag a block onto the lot. Edges snap together like Lego.

Drag a room from the left, or move the starter blocks. Drag porch and patio handles to shorten or extend them. Click a wall to select it, then press Delete or Remove wall. Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan. R rotates, Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates.
Place a room on the lot to preview it in 3D.
